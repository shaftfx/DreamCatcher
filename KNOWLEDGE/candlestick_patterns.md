# Candlestick Patterns — quick reference

Distilled from the user's *Technical Analysis Mastery* course, Section 1.

Use these as **timing confirmation** at potential entry zones (support/resistance, trend lines, prior breakout retests). Do NOT trade candles in isolation — they need a context.

A "candle" reads as: open / high / low / close. Body = open↔close range; wicks = high above body, low below body.

---

## Single-candle reversal signals

### Hammer 🔨 *(bullish reversal)*
- Small body at the top, **long lower wick** ≥ 2× body.
- After a downtrend → buyers rejected lower prices.
- Confirmation: next candle closes above the hammer's high.

### Inverted Hammer *(bullish reversal)*
- Small body at the bottom, long upper wick ≥ 2× body.
- After a downtrend → buyers attempted up but were rejected. Still bullish if next candle is green.

### Hanging Man *(bearish reversal)*
- Looks like a hammer, but appears at the **top** of an uptrend.
- Long lower wick = sellers tested down. Bearish if next candle is red and closes below it.

### Shooting Star *(bearish reversal)*
- Looks like inverted hammer, at the **top** of an uptrend.
- Long upper wick = buyers exhausted. Bearish if next candle confirms.

### Doji *(indecision)*
- Open ≈ close. Body is a thin line.
- At extremes: potential reversal. In a range: continuation.
- Always needs confirmation.

### Spinning Top *(indecision)*
- Small body, wicks on both sides roughly equal.
- Like a doji but slightly more directional. Same rule: needs confirmation.

### Marubozu *(strong continuation)*
- Big body, **no wicks** (or tiny). Bullish marubozu = open at low, close at high.
- Strong intraday conviction. Continuation signal.

## Two-candle reversal signals

### Bullish Engulfing
- Day 1: small red candle. Day 2: large green candle whose body **completely engulfs** day 1's body.
- After a downtrend → strong reversal.

### Bearish Engulfing
- Mirror: small green then large red engulfing.
- After an uptrend → strong reversal.

### Bullish Harami
- Day 1: large red candle. Day 2: small green candle inside day 1's body.
- After downtrend, signals weakening selling pressure.

### Bearish Harami
- Mirror: large green then small red inside.
- After uptrend, signals weakening buying.

---

## Three-candle reversal signals

### Morning Star *(bullish)*
- Day 1: large red. Day 2: small body (gap down). Day 3: large green closing into day 1's body.
- Classic bottom reversal.

### Evening Star *(bearish)*
- Mirror: large green, small body, large red. Top reversal.

### Three White Soldiers *(bullish continuation/reversal)*
- Three consecutive long green candles with each opening within the prior body and closing near its high.
- Strong bullish momentum.

### Three Black Crows *(bearish)*
- Mirror: three long red candles with the same structure.

---

## How Bull uses these

- After identifying a fundamental + catalyst-driven candidate (`STRATEGY.md`), look at the **daily** chart of the last 30 bars.
- If the most recent candle is one of: bullish engulfing, hammer, morning star, or three white soldiers — and price is above the 50-day SMA — that's a green light for entry timing.
- If the most recent candle is one of: bearish engulfing, shooting star, evening star, three black crows — even if fundamentals are good, **wait** for the technical to clear.
- A doji or spinning top at a key level is a "watch-and-wait" — recheck next routine.

Never enter on a single candle alone. The pattern is timing on top of an already-formed thesis.
